## 0. How to become an administrator
> 1️⃣Use your Hong Kong mobile phone number to register as an **investor** in **Virtu Capital Client**, and bind a custom email address such as: name@virtu-capital.com and password (the current email address does not require registration and verification, and verification will be added later)
> 2️⃣Change the investor role to administrator from the administrator account;
> 3️⃣ Change the investor role to super administrator by the back-end technician.
