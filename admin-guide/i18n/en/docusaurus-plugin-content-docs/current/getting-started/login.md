## 1. System login and basic settings

### 1.1 Log in to the system

**Operation path**: Visit the administrator website → Enter the account password → Click to log in

| Steps | Operating Instructions |
|------|---------|
| 1 | Open Virtu Capital [Administrator address](https://app.virtu-capital.com/zh-Hant/admin/users) in your browser |
| 2 | Enter **email account** (such as admin@virtucapital.com) |
| 3 | Enter **password** |
| 4 | Click the "Login" button |

> ⚠️ **Security Tip**: Please change the initial password immediately after logging in for the first time. It is recommended to turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).

![](../assets/01-login-page.jpg)


### 1.2 Interface after login

After successful login, the lower left corner of the page displays:
- **Administrator Name**: The display name of the current login account
- **Logout button**: Click to exit the system safely

| 1 | You can choose to switch **language** (support English/Traditional Chinese/Simplified Chinese) |
|---|---|
| 2 | Option to switch **theme** (Dark / Light mode) | |
