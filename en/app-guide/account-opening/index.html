Personal Account Opening

After registering the app account, follow the in-app workflow to apply for a securities account.

Before you start​

Prepare:

  • Your own valid, unexpired identity card or passport.
  • Your residential and correspondence addresses.
  • Your tax-residency, employment, financial, asset, and investment information.

Fields vary by document jurisdiction, occupation, tax status, and account selections. Follow the current page instead of copying example values.

1. Open the application and accept the agreements​

On Assets, tap Open Account. If the account-type page appears, select Personal account.

Read the Client Agreement and Client Declarations, check the acknowledgement, then tap Agree and continue.

2. Complete identity verification and upload a document​

2.1 Review the KYC workflow​

The Sumsub-supported verification overview covers identity documents, personal information, employment and financial information, asset and investment information, compliance information, and live authentication.

Third-party verification page

Identity verification is completed inside a third-party service page. Grant camera access only through the official workflow and do not send identity photos through chat or an unofficial link.

2.2 Select and photograph the document​

  1. Choose the issuing country or region.
  2. Select the matching document type, such as ID card or passport.
  3. Place the complete document in the camera frame and photograph every requested side or page.
  4. Check orientation and clarity. Choose Document is readable only when the text, portrait, and edges can be seen.
Document-photo requirements

Use your own genuine, valid document. Avoid obstruction, cropped edges, blur, glare, or a photo of another screen. Complete any back side or passport data page requested by the workflow.

3. Review and enter personal information​

Complete the fields shown for:

  • Full name, first name, last name, gender, education, and date of birth.
  • Nationality, document type and number, expiry, and issuing authority.
  • Country or region of residence, residential address, and correspondence address.
  • Any additional tax or contact information requested for your selections.
Use your real information

The name, identity number, and birth date must match the document. Enter genuine address and tax-residency information; never copy the names, numbers, or addresses shown in an example.

4. Complete the account-opening questionnaire​

4.1 Employment and financial information​

Choose the occupation that matches your current situation. Employed generally asks for the complete company name, jurisdiction, address, business nature, position, work experience, tax-filing country or region, tax ID, phone, and email.

Different occupations display different required fields:

Enter every required field shown after changing the occupation.

Tax identification number

Choose the actual tax-filing jurisdiction before entering the identifier issued to you there.

  • For a Hong Kong individual completing CRS/AEOI self-certification, the TIN is the Hong Kong identity-card number, including its real check character but without parentheses.
  • A Mainland China resident with a PRC identity card generally uses the 18-character citizen identity number; the last character can be X.
  • A US individual, where applicable, enters the actual nine-digit SSN or ITIN.
  • For another jurisdiction, use the TIN issued by its tax authority. If none is issued, follow the reason-without-TIN option on the page or obtain professional guidance.

Declare every applicable tax-residency jurisdiction separately when required. Do not invent an identifier or substitute a passport number unless the relevant authority treats it as the TIN.

4.2 Asset and investment information​

Confirm the account type, then provide genuine information about source of funds and wealth, annual income, net assets, investment objective, monthly trading frequency, risk tolerance, investment experience, and any derivatives knowledge requested by the page.

How the information is used

Income, assets, sources, objectives, risk tolerance, and experience are used in account review and suitability assessment.

4.3 Confirm compliance information​

Read and answer every declaration, including beneficial ownership, US status or indicia, higher-risk business activity, politically exposed person status or relationships, W-8BEN generation, marketing channels, and client declarations.

If a declaration is unclear, contact customer support or a professional adviser rather than guessing.

5. Complete live authentication and KYC review​

Place your face inside the frame and follow the requested actions. After submission, the status page reviews each section and eventually shows Completed.

KYC completion is not final account approval

Completed means the current identity-verification workflow is finished. You must still review and sign the account-opening documents and wait for Virtu Capital’s manual review.

6. Preview and sign the account-opening documents​

6.1 Open documents pending signature​

When Assets shows Documents pending signature, tap Go to sign. Preview the generated personal application and check the identity, account, employment, and financial declarations.

Do not sign if the account number, identity number, birth date, phone, email, or address is incorrect.

6.2 Sign electronically​

The signature page switches to landscape. Sign personally inside the box, use Clear if necessary, and tap Submit only when the signature is correct.

The signature is applied to the required documents listed for this application.

6.3 Confirm signing and wait for review​

Return to the list and confirm that the personal application, CRS, W-8BEN, and any other required documents show Signed.

Use the current app status and in-app notices for the review result. If additional or corrected information is requested, re-enter the corresponding step from the official workflow instead of creating a duplicate application.

Frequently asked questions​

Why do different users see different fields?​

Document jurisdiction and type, occupation, tax status, and investment selections can change later fields. Complete the required fields actually shown on your page.

What if the document photo is rejected?​

Check camera permission, network connectivity, and document validity, then retake a complete, sharp, glare-free image. Contact customer support if repeated attempts fail or the status is unclear.

Why is the account still unavailable after KYC?​

KYC is followed by document signing and manual review. Securities services become available only after the app shows account approval and enables the relevant functions.
