View and Receive Statements

Statements are account records used to reconcile trades and positions and may also be required for a position-transfer application.

1. Open My Statements​

Open Profile → My Statements. The statement account UID appears at the top.

2. Filter statements​

Use the left menu to choose all, daily, or monthly statements. Use the right menu to choose a month, then wait for the list to finish loading.

  • Daily statement: covers one trading day.
  • Monthly statement: summarizes a longer monthly period.

If the list is empty, check the statement type and month and allow it to load before repeatedly changing filters.

3. Preview and receive a statement​

Tap a statement and check its type, date, UID, and format. A PDF can be:

  • Saved locally to the current device.
  • Sent by email to the address confirmed on the page.

These options only choose how to receive the existing statement; they do not generate a new statement.

Protect statement files

Statements contain account and asset information. Store them securely and share them only with a verified broker or platform page when required for a transfer.
