# Virtu Capital Position Transfer Services Agreement

**Version:** V1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** 3 August 2026  
**Last Updated:** 3 August 2026

> **Important Notice**  
> This Agreement is legally binding. By selecting “I have read and agree”, submitting a Position Transfer Application, signing a transfer form, or otherwise accepting this Agreement through the Virtu Capital application, website, or another designated channel, the Client confirms that the Client has read, understood, and agreed to all of its terms. Particular attention should be given to the provisions concerning information authorisation, asset restrictions, charges, processing times, cancellation, third-party processing, limitations of liability, and dispute resolution.

## 1. Parties and Scope

1. This Position Transfer Services Agreement (the **“Agreement”**) is entered into between:
   - **Service Provider:** Virtu Capital Finance Limited (**“Virtu Capital”**, **“we”**, **“us”**, or the **“Company”**); and
   - **Client:** the individual or entity that maintains a valid account with Virtu Capital and applies to use the Position Transfer Services (**“Client”** or **“you”**).
2. This Agreement applies when securities are transferred from another broker or custodian into a Virtu Capital account (an **“Inbound Transfer”**) or from a Virtu Capital account to another broker or custodian (an **“Outbound Transfer”**). Together, these are the **“Position Transfer Services”**.
3. This Agreement forms part of the Client’s existing client agreement, account terms, risk disclosures, charges displayed in the App, privacy policy, and other applicable documents. For matters specific to the Position Transfer Services, this Agreement prevails. Other matters are governed by the client agreement and any applicable specific terms. Applicable law, regulatory requirements, and relevant trading, custody, and settlement rules prevail in all cases.
4. This Agreement does not apply to internal securities transfers between Virtu Capital clients, securities trading, cash transfers, financing, or any service not expressly included in the Position Transfer Services.

## 2. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise:

1. **“Securities”** means shares and other investment products that the Company accepts for transfer from time to time.
2. **“Delivering Institution”** means the broker, custodian, or other financial institution delivering Securities to Virtu Capital in an Inbound Transfer.
3. **“Receiving Institution”** means the broker, custodian, or other financial institution receiving Securities from Virtu Capital in an Outbound Transfer.
4. **“Third-Party Institution”** includes any Delivering Institution, Receiving Institution, custodian, agent, exchange, clearing system, depository, or other service provider involved in a transfer.
5. **“Business Day”** means a day, other than a Saturday, Sunday, or public holiday in Auckland, on which registered banks are generally open for business in Auckland, New Zealand. Where an overseas market or Third-Party Institution is involved, its local business days may also affect processing time.
6. **“CCASS”** means the Central Clearing and Settlement System in Hong Kong, and **“DTC”** means The Depository Trust Company or its relevant settlement system in the United States.

## 3. Eligibility and General Conditions

1. When applying for a Position Transfer, the Client must:
   - have completed all account-opening, identity-verification, customer due-diligence, and ongoing-review requirements imposed by the Company;
   - maintain an account in good standing, without restrictions, freezes, investigations, unpaid liabilities, or other conditions that may prevent the transfer;
   - hold lawful, complete, and transferable rights in the relevant Securities and have authority to give the transfer instruction;
   - ensure that the account-holder information at the Delivering Institution, Virtu Capital, and the Receiving Institution is consistent, unless the Company has accepted another arrangement in writing; and
   - comply with applicable law, regulatory requirements, market rules, and Third-Party Institution requirements.
2. The Company will only process transfers involving supported markets, Securities, account types, and transfer methods. Availability is determined by the App, Company notices, operational capability, and the outcome of the Company’s review.
3. The standard process supports whole units only and does not support fractional units. A Position Transfer concerns Securities only and does not transfer cash, unless the Company expressly states otherwise.
4. The Company may reject, suspend, split, or require amendments to any application for compliance, risk-management, operational, third-party, security-status, or other reasonable grounds.

## 4. Client Application and Authority

1. The Client must follow the designated application process and provide complete, true, accurate, valid, and current information, including:
   - the Client’s name or entity name, Virtu Capital user identifier (VC UID), and account information;
   - transfer direction, market, Security name, Security code, and quantity;
   - the full name and CCASS, DTC, or other settlement identifier of the Delivering Institution or Receiving Institution;
   - the Client’s account type, account name, and account number at the relevant institution;
   - the relevant institution’s contact person, telephone number, and email address; and
   - the signed transfer form, recent account statement, and any other document required by the Company or a Third-Party Institution.
2. The Client authorises the Company to:
   - take all reasonably necessary steps to complete the transfer in accordance with the application and this Agreement;
   - send, verify, correct, or supplement transfer instructions and communicate with Third-Party Institutions regarding processing;
   - collect, use, retain, and disclose Client information for identity verification, compliance review, reconciliation, charges, dispute handling, and completion of the transfer; and
   - require the Client to re-sign documents, provide additional information, or otherwise confirm an instruction where reasonably necessary.
3. An electronic signature, electronic acceptance, verification code, or electronic submission has the same effect as a handwritten signature, except where mandatory law requires otherwise.
4. The Client must submit a corresponding instruction to the Delivering Institution or Receiving Institution. Any inconsistency in names, account numbers, Security codes, quantities, or other details may result in rejection, delay, or failure.
5. The Company is not required to assess the commercial purpose, tax consequences, or suitability of a transfer and does not provide investment, legal, or tax advice in connection with it.

## 5. Inbound Transfers

1. For an Inbound Transfer, the Client must ensure that the Delivering Institution receives a matching transfer-out instruction and must complete all procedures required by that institution.
2. The Company may conduct preliminary review, secondary review, broker reconciliation, settlement verification, and other necessary procedures. A status such as “submitted” or “processing” only confirms receipt or ongoing processing; it does not guarantee acceptance or crediting of the Securities.
3. An Inbound Transfer is complete only after the Company or its designated custodian has confirmed actual receipt, completed reconciliation, and credited the Securities to the Client’s account. The Client must not assume that Securities are available for sale, transfer, pledge, or other disposal before they appear as available in the account.
4. The Client must verify the name, code, quantity, and cost information of transferred Securities. If cost, acquisition date, or historical information is not transmitted, the Client must provide supporting evidence when requested. The Company may omit information while it is unavailable or under review. Cost information is maintained for record and reference purposes only; the Client remains responsible for original records and tax reporting.

## 6. Outbound Transfers

1. For an Outbound Transfer, the Client must hold sufficient transferable Securities and sufficient available funds for any charges, third-party costs, taxes, or other amounts due.
2. After submission, the Company may restrict or freeze the relevant Securities and may reserve or deduct estimated charges. While restricted, the Client may not sell, re-transfer, pledge, or otherwise dispose of those Securities.
3. Charges, asset values, and other amounts displayed in the App may be estimates. Actual amounts are determined by the Company’s final calculation and charges imposed by Third-Party Institutions.
4. If the Client has insufficient funds, the Company may suspend or reject the application, require additional funds, or deduct an amount from the Client’s available balance where permitted by the client agreement and applicable law. The Company will not sell Securities solely to pay transfer charges unless the Client gives a separate valid instruction or the client agreement expressly authorises it.
5. An Outbound Transfer is complete only after the Securities have been debited from the Client’s account, received by the relevant Third-Party Institution, and settled. The timing of credit by the Receiving Institution is outside the Company’s control.

## 7. Charges, Taxes, and Other Costs

1. Whether a transfer is subject to charges, and the applicable items, amounts, currencies, deduction times, and conditions, will be shown in the App or applicable instructions when the Client submits the application. This Agreement does not imply that the Company will charge a Position Transfer service fee.
2. Third-Party Institutions may impose custody, settlement, transfer, agency, tax, or other charges. Responsibility for those amounts is governed by the App, applicable instructions, and the actual charges imposed.
3. If the App requires payment or a reserve when the application is submitted, the Client must maintain sufficient available funds. Estimated and final charges may differ.
4. Any refund is subject to the conditions, amount, method, and timing stated in the App or applicable instructions and the actual processing outcome.
5. Costs arising from currency conversion, banking charges, or tax obligations will be borne by the responsible party in accordance with applicable law, App instructions, and actual processing. The Client should obtain professional advice where appropriate.

## 8. Processing Time and Third-Party Dependencies

1. Where information is complete, instructions match, and no exception applies, a transfer will ordinarily take approximately **five to ten Business Days**. This is an estimate, not a commitment or guarantee.
2. Completion may be affected by Third-Party Institutions, clearing systems, market holidays, corporate actions, trading suspensions, security restrictions, additional-information requests, regulatory review, system failure, force majeure, or other matters outside the Company’s reasonable control.
3. The Company may process multiple Securities in batches. Completion of one Security does not guarantee completion of another.
4. The Client must monitor progress through the designated channel and respond promptly to requests for information or confirmation. The Client is responsible for consequences caused by delayed responses.

## 9. Corporate Actions and Trading Risks

1. Dividends, rights issues, placements, consolidations, splits, takeovers, voting, subscriptions, redemptions, or other corporate actions may occur while a transfer is pending. Confirmation, allocation, and crediting of entitlements may be delayed or adjusted due to record dates, settlement status, and third-party processing.
2. The relevant Securities may be unavailable for trading during the transfer, their market price may change, and the Client may lose an opportunity or suffer loss as a result.
3. The Company will process corporate-action entitlements according to its records, information from Third-Party Institutions, applicable market rules, and entitlements actually received. The Client must provide any required instruction promptly; a failure to do so may cause rights to lapse.
4. Before applying, the Client must consider unsettled trades, short positions, margin requirements, liens, pledges, corporate actions, and tax consequences.

## 10. Cancellation, Amendment, Rejection, and Termination

1. If the App offers a cancellation function, the Client may request cancellation before the Company has executed the transfer or sent an irrevocable instruction to a Third-Party Institution. Cancellation is not guaranteed.
2. The Company may reject cancellation or amendment after reconciliation, delivery, settlement, or another irreversible stage has begun.
3. Following successful cancellation, the release of Securities or funds may require a reasonable processing period. The Client must wait until the relevant asset appears as available before trading or applying again.
4. The Company may reject, suspend, cancel, or terminate an application where:
   - information is incomplete, inaccurate, inconsistent, expired, or suspected to be false;
   - the Client fails to provide information, confirmation, or funds on time;
   - Securities are insufficient, non-transferable, restricted, frozen, pledged, or disputed;
   - the Client’s account is restricted, under investigation, or in arrears;
   - a Third-Party Institution rejects the transfer, cannot match it, or does not respond within a reasonable period;
   - the Company reasonably suspects fraud, money laundering, sanctions evasion, market misconduct, or other unlawful activity;
   - required by law, a regulator, a court, an exchange, a clearing system, or another competent authority; or
   - continuing would, in the Company’s reasonable opinion, create unacceptable legal, compliance, or operational risk.
5. The Company will notify the Client of the outcome where legally permitted and reasonably practicable, but need not disclose a reason where disclosure is prohibited.
