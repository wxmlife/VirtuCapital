# Corporate Account Opening

Corporate account opening is completed with assistance from Virtu Capital staff. Use the phone number or support email shown on the corporate account page in the app.

## 1. Open the corporate account page

On **Choose an account type**, select **Enterprise account** to open the contact page.

## 2. Contact the account-opening team

Use the phone number or email shown on that page, then provide company information and documents only as instructed by the account-opening team.

:::info Use the current contact details in the app
Support details and service hours may change. Always use the information currently displayed in the app.
:::

:::warning Send documents only through verified channels
Required documents vary by jurisdiction, company type, and account needs. Follow the staff member’s instructions and do not send company or personal sensitive information through an unofficial contact.
:::
