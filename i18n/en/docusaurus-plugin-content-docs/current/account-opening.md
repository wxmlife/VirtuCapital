# Account opening and identity authentication

After the registered mobile phone number is successfully verified, first set your nickname, email, login password and six-digit transaction password, and then read and agree to the user agreement and privacy policy.

![Set account profile and password](./assets/opening/01-after-registration.png)

After the account registration is completed, continue to follow the page to enter the account opening and identity authentication process:

### 1. Select account opening type

After registering your mobile phone number and verifying the verification code, select a personal or business account.

### 2. Confirm the account opening agreement

Read and confirm the account opening agreement.

### 3. Complete identity authentication

Upload your ID document and follow the prompts on the page to complete the liveness/identity verification.

### 4. Fill out the account opening questionnaire

Fill in your personal, professional and financial information.

### 5. Submit for review

Submit account opening information and check the review status on the page.
