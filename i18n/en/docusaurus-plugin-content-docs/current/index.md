# APP Beginner’s Guide

Find instructions in order of tasks. When using it for the first time, it is recommended to start with "Login and Account Opening"; users who have opened an account can directly enter the assets, trading or market modules.

| Account Start | Asset Management |
| --- | --- |
| [Login and Registration](./guides/login)<br/><small>Log in with mobile phone number verification code or email password</small> | [View account assets](./guides/assets)<br/><small>Balance, positions, profit and loss and quick entry</small> |
| [Account Opening and Identity Verification](./account-opening)<br/><small>Agreement, documents, questionnaire and review status</small> | [International Wire Deposit](./guides/deposit-international)<br/><small>Fill in the amount, obtain the collection account and submit the voucher</small> |
| [Account and Security](./account-and-security)<br/><small>Personal information, login password and transaction password</small> | [International Wire Withdrawal](./guides/withdraw-international)<br/><small>Select a bank account, confirm the fee and submit the application</small> |
| [Language and Preferences](./guides/settings)<br/><small>Language, price color, cache and other settings</small> | [Stock Transfer and Position Transfer](./guides/position-transfer)<br/><small>Transfer in, transfer out and intra-platform stock transfer</small> |

| Stock Trading | Market Exploration |
| --- | --- |
| [Manage Watchlist](./guides/watchlist)<br/><small>Add, remove and filter your selections</small> | [View Market Quotes](./guides/market)<br/><small>Indexes, popular stocks and gainer lists</small> |
| [View Stock Details](./guides/stock-detail)<br/><small>Quotes, candlesticks, transactions and orders</small> | [Search Stocks](./guides/market)<br/><small>Search securities by name or code</small> |
| [Buy and Sell Stocks](./guides/trade-stocks)<br/><small>Price, quantity, fees and order status</small> | [Stock Selection Basics](./education/stock-selection-basics)<br/><small>Build checklists from business, finance, valuation and risk</small> |
| [View and Cancel Orders](./guides/orders)<br/><small>Filter orders, view details and cancel orders</small> | [Market Indicators](./education/stock-selection-basics#market-indicators)<br/><small>Price, volume, market capitalization and valuation indicators</small> |

| Funding and Order Records | Help and Feedback |
| --- | --- |
| [Check Deposit Status](./guides/deposit-international#6-check-deposit-status)<br/><small>Confirm application, review and payment status</small> | [Submit Feedback](./guides/feedback)<br/><small>Fill in the problem description and attach relevant screenshots</small> |
| [Check Withdrawal Status](./guides/withdraw-international)<br/><small>Confirm application progress, fees and processing results</small> | [FAQ](./faq)<br/><small>Common instructions for accounts, funds, transactions and transfers</small> |

:::tip Can’t find the entrance?
Different account statuses, regions and APP versions may display different entrances. Please confirm that account opening has been completed first, and refer to the current page of the APP.
