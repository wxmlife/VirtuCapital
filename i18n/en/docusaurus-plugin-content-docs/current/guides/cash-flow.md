# View Cash Flow

Cash Flow helps you reconcile balance changes caused by trades and account services.

## 1. Open Cash Flow

Open **Assets → Cash Flow**.

![Cash Flow page](../assets/en/cash-flow/01-cash-flow.jpeg)

## 2. Review income and expenses

The summary at the top shows **Total income** and **Total expense** in the selected reporting currency. Each record includes the business type or stock, time, processing status, amount, and currency.

A plus or minus sign shows the direction of the balance change. Always read it together with the record type:

- Buying stock and withdrawing funds usually create an expense.
- Selling stock usually creates income.
- Stock transfers and position transfers appear according to their actual processing result.

![A completed trade shown in Cash Flow](../assets/en/cash-flow/02-trade-record.png)

## 3. Filter records

Choose **All, Deposit, Withdraw, Buy, Sell**, or another available type. After filtering, still verify the date, currency, and status. Do not treat a failed or processing item as a completed balance change.

:::warning A Deposit filter does not mean deposits are currently available
The page may retain a Deposit filter for historical records or different account states. The current app does not support creating a new deposit request. Follow the business entries shown on your Assets page and the latest platform notices.
:::
