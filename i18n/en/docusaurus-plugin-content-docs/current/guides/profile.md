# Open the Profile Page

![Profile page](../assets/en/profile/01-profile.jpeg)

The Profile page shows your nickname, VC UID, and verification status. It also provides access to:

- **Account info**: view and maintain your account details.
- **My Statements**: filter, download, or email daily and monthly statements.
- **User Agreement and Privacy Policy**: read the current legal documents.
- **Feedback**: submit business or product feedback.
- **Settings**: change the language, price colors, and other preferences.

The envelope in the upper-right corner opens the Message Center. A red dot means that you have unread messages. Use the Message Center to review trading, asset, security, and system notices.

When copying your VC UID or providing it to customer support, check the complete number before sending it.

See also: [View the Message Center](./messages).

:::tip Check your verification status first
Some asset and trading functions are available only after account opening and identity verification are complete. If an entry is missing, check the verification status shown on this page.
:::
