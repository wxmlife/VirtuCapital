# Stock transfer within the platform

Intra-platform stock transfer is different from inter-brokerage transfer, which is to transfer the stocks in the Virtu Capital account to another Virtu Capital user.

### 1. Enter the stock transfer page

Go to "Assets → Stock Transfer".

![In-platform stock transfer page](../assets/09_stock_transfer.png)

### 2. Select recipient and stock

Search for the recipient's UID, mobile phone number or email address in "Transfer to". Search and select the stock to confirm the transferable quantity.

### 3. Fill in the transfer information

Select the transaction type, fill in the quantity and agreed price.

### 4. Submit application

Read the risk statement, check the box to confirm, and click "Submit Stock Transfer Application". After submission, save the transaction code generated by the system and check the review status on the record page.

:::warning Check before submission
Please confirm the recipient's identity, stock code, quantity and agreed price. Do not create the same application again after submission to avoid repeated review.
:::
