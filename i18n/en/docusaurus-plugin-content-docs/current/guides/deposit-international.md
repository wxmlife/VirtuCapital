# Deposit Availability

:::warning Deposits are not currently supported in the app
Do not remit funds using an old screenshot, bank account, or obsolete workflow.
:::

To add eligible securities assets, use:

- [Position Transfer](./position-transfer) to transfer stocks from your own account at another broker.
- [Stock Transfer](./stock-transfer) to receive stocks from another eligible Virtu Capital client under the platform rules.

The former deposit workflow is intentionally not shown in the current guide. Detailed instructions will return only after the feature, receiving account, and complete workflow have been verified again.
