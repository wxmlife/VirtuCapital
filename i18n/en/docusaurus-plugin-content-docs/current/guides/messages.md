# View the Message Center

The Message Center collects trading, asset, security, and system notices.

## 1. Open the Message Center

On the **Profile** page, tap the envelope in the upper-right corner. A red dot means that unread messages are available.

## 2. Filter messages

Use **All, Trading, Assets, Security**, or **System** to filter the list. The page shows the unread count and can mark all messages as read.

## 3. Check service progress

Position transfers and stock transfers may send notices such as submitted, preliminary review passed, approved, or completed. When opening a message, compare its application number with the corresponding record in the app. Do not rely only on the notification title.

## 4. Respond to security notices

If you receive a password-change or unusual-sign-in notice:

1. Confirm whether you performed the action.
2. If not, change both your sign-in and trading passwords immediately.
3. Never disclose a verification code or password, and contact customer support promptly.

:::info Verify the business record
Use the application number, account information, and business record shown in your own account as the source of truth. A message title does not replace the final processing result.
:::
