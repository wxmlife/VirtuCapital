# App Beginner’s Guide

Find instructions by task. New users should register and sign in first, then complete account opening. Approved users can go directly to assets, quotes, or trading.

| Getting started | Asset services |
| --- | --- |
| [Registration and Sign-In](/app-guide/guides/login)<br/><small>Register first, then use a phone code or email and password</small> | [View Account Assets](/app-guide/guides/assets)<br/><small>Balances, positions, profit or loss, and service entries</small> |
| [Personal Account Opening](/app-guide/account-opening)<br/><small>Agreements, identity documents, questionnaire, signatures, and review</small> | [Deposit Availability](/app-guide/guides/deposit-international)<br/><small>Deposits are unavailable; use a position transfer or stock transfer</small> |
| [Corporate Account Opening](/app-guide/corporate-account-opening)<br/><small>Contact the account-opening team through the current app page</small> | [International Wire Withdrawal](/app-guide/guides/withdraw-international)<br/><small>Bank details, amount, fees, submission, and status</small> |
| [Account and Security](/app-guide/account-and-security)<br/><small>Profile, sign-in password, and trading password</small> | [Transfer Positions In and Out](/app-guide/guides/position-transfer)<br/><small>Preparation, four-step applications, progress, and cancellation</small> |
| [Using the Profile Page](/app-guide/guides/profile)<br/><small>Account info, statements, feedback, settings, and messages</small> | [Transfer Stock to Another User](/app-guide/guides/stock-transfer)<br/><small>MIT, OTC, BSN, freezing, and cancellation</small> |
| [Language and Preferences](/app-guide/guides/settings)<br/><small>Language, price colors, cache, and version information</small> | [View Cash Flow](/app-guide/guides/cash-flow)<br/><small>Balance changes from trades and account services</small> |
| [View the Message Center](/app-guide/guides/messages)<br/><small>Trading, asset, security, and system notices</small> | [View and Receive Statements](/app-guide/guides/statements)<br/><small>Filter, save, or email daily and monthly statements</small> |

| Trading and quotes | Research and orders |
| --- | --- |
| [Manage Watchlist](/app-guide/guides/watchlist)<br/><small>Search, add, remove, and filter stocks</small> | [View Market Quotes](/app-guide/guides/market)<br/><small>HK and US indices, popular stocks, and initial review</small> |
| [View Stock Details](/app-guide/guides/stock-detail)<br/><small>Quotes, charts, trades, order book, and fund data</small> | [Stock Selection Basics](/app-guide/education/stock-selection-basics)<br/><small>Build a checklist around business, finances, valuation, and risk</small> |
| [Buy and Sell Stocks](/app-guide/guides/trade-stocks)<br/><small>Side, price, quantity, validity, fees, and status</small> | [Market Indicators](/app-guide/education/stock-selection-basics#market-indicators)<br/><small>Price, volume, market capitalization, and valuation</small> |
